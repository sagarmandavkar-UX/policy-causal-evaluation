# Executive memo

## Decision question

Did the simulated regional policy reduce the measured outcome after accounting for persistent regional differences and common time shocks?

## Finding

The estimated average effect is approximately **-3.10 units**, with a 95% confidence interval of **-3.59 to -2.61**. The differential pre-period trend test is not significant at 5% in the seeded demonstration.

## Recommendation

Treat this as evidence that the implementation pattern is capable of recovering the planted effect—not as real policy evidence. Before a real recommendation, replace the fixture with authoritative public data and complete spillover, concurrent-policy, and alternative-comparison sensitivity checks.
